CLAUDE:
Exactly right, Godfred. By default, `<li>` items stack vertically, one per line. `display: inline` makes them sit side by side like words in a sentence, which is what turns your list into a horizontal menu. The `margin-right: 15px` then adds the breathing room between them.

One small refinement: the margin isn't what makes them horizontal. That's all `display: inline`. The margin just spaces them out once they're already in a row.

CLAUDE:
You're close, Godfred. The effect part is right: it adds an extra style to your links, but only at a specific moment.

Two small sharpenings. The `&` stands for the parent selector, so inside the `a` rule, `&:hover` means "`a` when the mouse is hovering over it."
